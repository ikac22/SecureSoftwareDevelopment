# Injection vulnerabilities extension

Ovaj dokument povezuje pet injekcionih slabosti analiziranih u repozitorijumu [master-thesis](https://github.com/ikac22/master-thesis) sa konkretnim funkcionalnostima ove aplikacije i eksperimentalnim granama na kojima su dokumentovani napadi i njihove mitigacije.

| CWE | Funkcionalnost i ranjivo mesto na `master` grani | Eksperimentalna grana | Dokumentacija na grani |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CWE-943 — NoSQL injection** | Pretraga istorije završenih servisa (`POST /api/my/service-history/search`). [`ServiceHistoryController`](src/main/java/com/zuehlke/securesoftwaredevelopment/controller/ServiceHistoryController.java) prihvata `filters` i `view` kao generičke mape, a [`ServiceHistorySearchRepository.search()`](src/main/java/com/zuehlke/securesoftwaredevelopment/repository/ServiceHistorySearchRepository.java) ih neposredno ugrađuje u MongoDB `$match` i `$project` (`putIfAbsent` / `putAll`). | [PR #25](https://github.com/ikac22/SecureSoftwareDevelopment/pull/25) · [`attack/cwe-943-nosql-service-history`](https://github.com/ikac22/SecureSoftwareDevelopment/tree/attack/cwe-943-nosql-service-history) | `attacks/cwe-943/attack-vulnerable.md` opisuje napad i izvršavanje istog eksperimenta nad ranjivim i mitigovanim snapshotom. Skripta je u `attacks/cwe-943/run-attack.sh`, a dokazi se generišu u `attacks/cwe-943/evidence/{vulnerable,mitigated}/`. |
| **CWE-78 — OS command injection / argument injection** | Selektivno preuzimanje dokumentacije završenog servisa (`POST /services/{serviceId}/documents/bundle`). U [`ServiceDocumentBundleService`](src/main/java/com/zuehlke/securesoftwaredevelopment/service/ServiceDocumentBundleService.java) korisničke vrednosti prolaze kroz `normalizeSelection()` i zatim se u `extractSelectedDocuments()` dodaju u listu argumenata programa `tar` bez jasne granice između operanada i opcija. | [PR #26](https://github.com/ikac22/SecureSoftwareDevelopment/pull/26) · [`attack/cwe-78-service-document-bundle`](https://github.com/ikac22/SecureSoftwareDevelopment/tree/attack/cwe-78-service-document-bundle) | `attacks/cwe-78/attack-vulnerable.md` i `attacks/cwe-78/attack-mitigated.md`; reprodukcija je u `attacks/cwe-78/run-attack.sh`, a dokazi u `attacks/cwe-78/evidence/`. |
| **CWE-90 — LDAP injection** | Pretraga servisera tokom dodele termina (`GET /services/{id}/available-slots?...&search=...`). [`LdapTechnicianDirectory.search()`](src/main/java/com/zuehlke/securesoftwaredevelopment/service/LdapTechnicianDirectory.java) tekst iz `search` parametra konkatenira direktno u LDAP filter za više atributa. | [PR #27](https://github.com/ikac22/SecureSoftwareDevelopment/pull/27) · [`attack/cwe-90-ldap-technician-search`](https://github.com/ikac22/SecureSoftwareDevelopment/tree/attack/cwe-90-ldap-technician-search) | `attacks/cwe-90/attack-vulnerable.md` i `attacks/cwe-90/attack-mitigated.md`; reprodukcija je u `attacks/cwe-90/run-attack.sh`, a generisani dokazi u `attacks/cwe-90/evidence/`. |
| **CWE-94 — Code injection** | Probni obračun popusta (`POST /loyalty-calculator`). [`ServicePricingPolicyEvaluator`](src/main/java/com/zuehlke/securesoftwaredevelopment/service/ServicePricingPolicyEvaluator.java) u `renderExpression()` tekstualno umeće `partnerCode` u SpEL izraz, koji se zatim prosleđuje `parseExpression()` i `getValue()`. | [PR #28](https://github.com/ikac22/SecureSoftwareDevelopment/pull/28) · [`attack/cwe-94-loyalty-spel`](https://github.com/ikac22/SecureSoftwareDevelopment/tree/attack/cwe-94-loyalty-spel) | `attacks/cwe-94/attack-vulnerable.md` i `attacks/cwe-94/attack-mitigated.md`; reprodukcija je u `attacks/cwe-94/run-attack.sh`, a dokazi u `attacks/cwe-94/evidence/`. |
| **CWE-610 — Externally Controlled Reference to a Resource** | Privatna galerija automobila (`GET /gallery/image?path=...` i `POST /my-gallery/upload`). [`PersonalGalleryService`](src/main/java/com/zuehlke/securesoftwaredevelopment/service/PersonalGalleryService.java) u `loadForDisplay()` normalizuje putanju bez provere da rezultat ostaje ispod `galleryRoot`, dok `store()` proverava postojanje cilja pre validacije korisničkog naziva. | [PR #29](https://github.com/ikac22/SecureSoftwareDevelopment/pull/29) · [`attack/cwe-610-personal-gallery-reference`](https://github.com/ikac22/SecureSoftwareDevelopment/tree/attack/cwe-610-personal-gallery-reference) | `attacks/cwe-610/attack-vulnerable.md` i `attacks/cwe-610/attack-mitigated.md`; reprodukcija je u `attacks/cwe-610/run-attack.sh`, a dokazi u `attacks/cwe-610/evidence/{vulnerable,mitigated}/`. |

Eksperimentalne grane čuvaju odvojene ranjive i mitigovane snapshotove i pokreću isti napad nad oba stanja, tako da se efekat mitigacije može neposredno uporediti sa ranjivom implementacijom.
